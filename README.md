# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description
A simple interest calculator that calculates the interest earned on a principal amount over a specific period of time.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

## Inputs
- Principal: The initial amount of money.
- Rate: The annual interest rate (percentage).
- Time: The duration in years.

## Example
- Principal: 1000
- Rate: 5%
- Time: 2 years

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100

## Result
The total simple interest is 100.
